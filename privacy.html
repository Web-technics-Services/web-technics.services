Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Web Technics processes when you visit this website or contact us.

Effective date: 13 September 2026

Who is responsible

Web Technics, operating from Cambodia, is responsible for the processing described in this policy. To ask a privacy question or exercise your rights, use the details on our contact page.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, telephone, WhatsApp, Telegram, or another service, we process the information you choose to provide so we can respond and manage the related business relationship.

The enquiry form on this website prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not store the form contents.

Website and analytics data

Our hosting provider may process technical request logs needed to deliver and secure the website. With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-MMMGPHD62R to understand website use. Depending on our Analytics settings, this can include visited pages, approximate location, referral source, browser and device information, and interactions such as scrolls, outbound clicks, site searches, video engagement, and file downloads.

Google Analytics uses first-party identifiers and may set cookies including _ga and _ga_<container-id>. Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses. Cookie duration depends on configuration and may be up to two years. Analytics does not load unless you accept it.

Why we process information

We process information to operate and secure the website, respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain business records, and understand and improve website performance. The lawful basis depends on the context and applicable law, including steps requested before a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests, and consent where required.

Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, communications, and analytics. Google may process Analytics data on infrastructure outside your country. Those providers process information under their own terms and applicable transfer safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution requirements. Analytics retention is controlled through our Google Analytics property settings. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. You can reject analytics without losing access to the website and change your choice at any time.

You can also restrict cookies through your browser and use Google's Analytics opt-out tools. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

External services and changes

Links to third-party websites and communication services are governed by their own privacy practices. We may update this policy when our services, providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
